# Options Greeks Learning Course - Complete Curriculum

## 📚 Course Overview

This revamped curriculum takes learners from first principles to advanced professional practice. It now spans **8 modules**, **50+ lessons**, interactive exercises, and a strategy library with professional-grade guidance and payoff visualizations.

**Total Duration:** 16–22 hours  
**Skill Level:** Beginner → Advanced  
**Prerequisites:** Basic familiarity with stocks and market mechanics

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. ✅ Explain the structure and lifecycle of options contracts
2. ✅ Read options chains and evaluate liquidity, IV, and positioning
3. ✅ Price options conceptually and understand Black-Scholes limits
4. ✅ Interpret core and second-order Greeks for risk management
5. ✅ Analyze volatility surfaces, skew, and term structure
6. ✅ Build, evaluate, and manage options strategies across regimes
7. ✅ Apply professional risk, sizing, and adjustment frameworks
8. ✅ Understand advanced modeling and real‑world market structure
